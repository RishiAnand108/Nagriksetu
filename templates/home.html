How it works

From a photo on the street to a closed complaint

Three steps for citizens, one accountable workflow for the ward office.

1

Report it

Choose the issue type, add a photo and let your phone share its location. A landmark helps when GPS is unavailable.

2

It reaches the right office

The complaint is routed to your ward. Corporators triage it by priority and assign it to someone responsible.

3

Track every step

Each status change is written to a timeline with who made it and why. You get an email and can reply on the complaint.
